Why people join

The club runs on a simple rhythm. Show up, work on the fundamentals, and come back next week.

Most people who walk in have not played seriously since school, and a few have never played at all. Sessions are built so that both of those people can be in the same gym without anyone feeling out of place. You get real coaching on the basics rather than being thrown straight into a game.

There are no tryouts and no season commitment. Pick one sport or move between them during the week, whichever keeps you coming.
