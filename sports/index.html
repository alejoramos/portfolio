The club runs soccer, basketball, running and fitness sessions. No tryouts, all levels, every week.

What this is

A club, not a team. Turning up
is the whole entry requirement.

We run sessions the way a good pickup game works. You show up, you get put with people at your level, a coach actually teaches you something, and then you play.

Nobody gets cut. Nobody sits on a bench. If you have never kicked a ball with any confidence, Tuesday is still for you.

Four sports.
One membership.

  1. 01

    Soccer

    Small sided games on turf. Ball control and movement first, then we play until it gets dark.

    Tuesdays, 5:30 to 7:00pm

  2. 02

    Basketball

    Shooting, passing and footwork, then full court games with the teams mixed up every week.

    Mondays, 5:00 to 6:30pm

  3. 03

    Running

    The group splits by pace, so nobody gets dropped and nobody has to hold back. Everyone finishes together.

    Thursdays, 6:00 to 7:00pm

  4. 04

    Fitness training

    Strength and conditioning that supports whatever else you are doing. Scaled to whoever turns up.

    Wednesdays, 4:30 to 5:30pm

The sport is
the excuse.

Half the people here joined for the exercise and stayed for the group chat. That is a completely acceptable reason to keep coming.

Why people
actually stay.

  1. Someone learns your name in week one

    Coaches run the same session every week, so they know who you are and what you are working on.

  2. You are never the worst person there

    Groups are split by level within the session. You train with people moving at your speed.

  3. Missing a week costs nothing

    No season, no commitment, no guilt. Come back on Tuesday and nobody mentions it.

  4. It is the same forty faces

    That is what turns a session into a reason to leave the house on a cold evening.
