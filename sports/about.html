Sports for all levels.

The club runs on simple coaching, consistent practice and the idea that the hardest part is walking in the first time.

What we are trying to do

Give people a place to be active that does not require already being good at something. Whether someone played through school or has never touched a ball, the session is built so both of them get something out of the same hour.

Coaches learn names in the first week. That sounds small, but it is most of the reason people keep coming back.

What we focus on

  • Basic skills and fundamentals
  • Teamwork and respect
  • Fitness and healthy habits
  • Confidence through practice
