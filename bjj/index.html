01  /  Method

Position
before
submission.

We teach in the order the art actually works. Where to put your body first, how to stay safe there, and only then how to finish.

Most people arrive expecting to be thrown into a fight. That is not what happens. The first month is spent learning to move on the ground, protect your neck, and get out from underneath someone without panicking. It is slow on purpose.

Classes are capped so an instructor can correct you by name. Nobody is left to copy the person next to them and hope.

03  /  Two ways to train

Gi and no-gi.
Start with either.

Gi

Grips, patience, and a lot of detail.

The jacket gives everyone something to hold, which slows the game down and rewards precision over speed. This is where most people learn fastest. We lend you a gi for the first month.

Mondays and Wednesdays

No-gi

Faster, sweatier, fewer places to hold.

Shorts and a rash guard. Without the jacket everything moves quicker and you learn to control with angles and pressure instead of grips. Bring what you already own.

Fridays and Saturday open mat

04  /  Why people stay

It changes
more than
your fitness.

  1. You learn to control a situation

    Position, distance and leverage, which turns out to be most of self defence. Nothing here depends on hitting anyone.

  2. The conditioning arrives on its own

    Nobody makes you do burpees. Strength and cardio show up because you spent ninety minutes moving under resistance.

  3. You get calmer under pressure

    Being stuck somewhere bad and thinking your way out is a skill. It transfers off the mat more than people expect.

  4. Everyone was new once

    You train with partners, not against them. The higher belts are the ones most careful with beginners.

Coming for the first time

Wear
Shorts without pockets or zips, and a t-shirt. We lend gis.
Bring
Water, and flip flops for walking off the mat.
Arrive
Ten minutes early so someone can walk you through it.
Expect
A warm up, one position, drilling, and rounds if you want them.

You will know
everyone’s name
by the third week.

That is not a promise about the training. It is just what happens when the same forty people keep turning up to the same room.
